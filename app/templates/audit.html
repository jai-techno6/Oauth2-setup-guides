Evidence audit

What was checked in the curated guides, how, and what changed as a result. The common view of a provider's difficulty was wrong more than once. Those corrections are listed here.

How checks were done

  • Doc fetch: the provider page was fetched on the date shown and the quoted text found on it. Claims checked this way are marked cited ✓.
  • Search snippet only: the claim appears in a search result excerpt from the page, but the full page could not be fetched (often HTTP 403 for automated clients). Marked cited, without the check mark.
  • Live probe: an HTTP request without credentials (discovery documents, console redirects). Marked tested.
  • Not done: no developer account was created and no OAuth app was registered or submitted for review. Hands-on times are estimates (inferred) until someone times a real setup.
